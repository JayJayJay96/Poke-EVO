# Rapid Spin — shared move

**Base:** `[Normal, Orbit, Contact]` · 3 shells orbit you for 2.5 s, stagger 15 px.
cooldown 4 s · 9 dmg · orbit 50 px. Data: `src/data/moves.ts` `rapidSpin`.
**Learned by:** Squirtle line.

## Generic Mutations
| Mutation | What changes | Cost |
|---|---|---|
| **Spiral Spin** `rapidSpin.spiralSpin` | The shells spiral outward from 30 px to 120 px over the duration, sweeping both the inner and the outer ring. | −15% damage. |
| **Deflector** `rapidSpin.deflector` | The shells destroy enemy projectiles they touch (ranged enemies arrive in P5); each one destroyed adds +0.3 s duration. | −30% damage. Worth more once ranged enemies exist. |
