# Augment capacity and reward economy

- Each skill has 3 behavioral slots. Mastery ranks 1,2,3 unlock slots; augments remain separate from numerical enhancements.
- **Eight protected baseline reward opportunities** during a reference 35-min completed run, target approximate minutes 3,6,9,13,17,21,26,31. Opportunity is not an automatically equipped augment. Staged scheduling may occur through events near those times and must support delayed catch-up.
- Four skills x three slots = maximum 12. Expected 7–9 equipped for completed runs, subject to actual eligibility and player choices.
- Side objectives and elite rewards supply bonus augment opportunities, earlier access, reroll currency or replacement token. Protect the eight baseline rewards from optional completion; cap total opportunities (provisionally 10–11) and test snowballing.

## Reward handling
1. Award an immutable augment ticket with its source ID and trigger order.
2. If any eligible unlocked free slot exists, generate a three-card draft for a valid target; permit focus weighting.
3. If no slot exists, retain a **pending** ticket (provisional pending storage cap 2). Next capacity unlock flushes queued tickets one-by-one.
4. At pending cap, convert subsequent opportunities to a guaranteed valid compensation (e.g. normal enhancement draft or one reroll token) and log conversion. Do not silently consume reward.
5. If all 12 slots occupied, additional augment tickets may offer replacement only with an available authorized token, otherwise explicit compensation.
6. Spending a replacement token must preview loss of active mutation or synergy; never drop an augment without acceptance.

## Player-facing acquisition
Use named augment milestone indicators and an actual choice screen; do not show a confusing third XP bar/currency unless tied to gameplay decisions. Allow early available skills to progress enough to use first rewards. Allow players to skip, but define explicit compensation and whether skip counts toward 8 baseline opportunities (yes: opportunity offered).
