# Progression philosophy and vocabulary

**Five player experiences:** acquire base skills; enhance numerical performance; equip 3-slot behavioral augments; choose a mutually exclusive evolution path and its ranks; optionally accept discovered mutations. **Two separate progressions:** Pokémon form evolution from character level; skill evolution from mastery eligibility + protected selection opportunities.

## Vocabulary
- **Character XP** drives level and normal level-up draft, and family form thresholds.
- **Mastery XP** is per-equipped-skill proof of qualified contribution; mastery unlocks capacity and evolution rank eligibility only.
- **Reward ticket/opportunity** is an internal, queued selection entitlement of type normal, augment, evolution, mutation; not a grindable visible currency.
- **Eligibility** means a candidate currently passes hard conditions; **weight** means how often eligible candidates appear. Never use weights to override eligibility.
- **Focus skill** changes relevant-offer probability without guaranteeing a recipe.
- **Mutation discovery** means requirements satisfied and an accepted or pending player decision, not a random stat roll.

## Principle: meaningful randomness
Guarantee baseline opportunity, not a predetermined exact build. Allow deliberate pursuit with focus, bounded rerolls, revealed recipes after discovery, and normal evolution branch selection. Test casual, specialist, synergy-hunter and experimental player strategies.
