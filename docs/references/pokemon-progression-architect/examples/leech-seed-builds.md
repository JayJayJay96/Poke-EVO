# Leech Seed build pursuit examples

## Specializing intentionally
- Learn Leech Seed early; select it as Focus Skill; pursue mastery 1–3 and equip two compatible behavioral augments.
- At mastery 4, first protected evolution ticket allows all valid branches: Parasitic Network / Living Garden / Bulbasaur-family Symbiotic Bloom.
- Pursue Living Garden + Momentum + Volatility. If the authored Wandering Blight recipe becomes eligible, queue a mutation choice instead of automatically transforming it.
- Reroll may improve odds without guaranteeing both augments. The player can accept a stationary Anchor alternative and make a different coherent build.

## Support skill mastery
Synthesis should gain mastery on *effective heal*, shield actually used, or qualified protective contribution. Zero credit for repeated full-health heals. A character that avoids damage entirely still needs a bounded support catch-up model or explicit protected rank eligibility so it is not punished for success; test carefully without granting infinite free mastery.

## Late Solar Beam
At minute 17, Solar Beam starts with provisional mastery floor 300 and x1.30 catch-up until peer rank threshold, but does not retroactively receive previous augment rewards. It may compete for subsequent tickets. This should be tested against remaining time and the probability of reaching mastery 4 before the final boss.
